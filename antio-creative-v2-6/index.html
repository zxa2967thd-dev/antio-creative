About Antio Creative

Built from a $250 camera.

Antio Creative started in college with a $250 camera, no formal training, and a determination to learn. Zack Antaya taught himself photography, editing, design, websites, social media, and marketing—building every skill and every opportunity from the ground up.

What began with local shoots grew into work for restaurants, consumer brands, and live events featuring nationally recognized talent. Today, Antio Creative brings that same self-taught drive to businesses that want stronger visuals, clearer messaging, and a brand people actually remember.

No shortcuts. No agency behind the scenes. Just years of learning, creating, and getting better with every project.
